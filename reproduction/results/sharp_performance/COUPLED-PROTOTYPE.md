# Coupled numerical continuation of the frozen task

The initial validation measurements expose a preparation bottleneck: with 2048 scalar contacts, the existing H² Schur route solves thousands of contact cross right-hand sides. It takes 57.67 seconds at 16384 validation normals with 8 iterations, versus 2.50 seconds for accuracy-passing plain float32 Cholesky. One H² iteration still costs 27.18 seconds and passes. The shorter-scale model does not need the dense correction required by the older smooth setting; that older baseline would therefore exaggerate a new H² advantage.

The coupled prototype solves the augmented scalar/vector Gaussian directly. Its observation covariance has three repeated normal blocks A, scalar prior block Kcc plus declared scalar noise, and the full cross block B. It shares the same normal preconditioner and hierarchy or locality operator. For128 queries it solves129 augmented right-hand sides (queries plus data), corresponding to387 normal-block columns, rather than6531 normal-block columns for the Schur preparation/query calculation. Every contact remains in the same Gaussian model.

The initial block-diagonal preconditioner uses normal AFN and the scalar PRIOR Cholesky. A stronger symmetric block-triangular variant also applies the cross block in the preconditioner, retaining the scalar prior factor as an approximate Schur factor. A diagonal-Schur trial is retained as a failed numerical setting; its positive diagonal does not capture the important scalar correlations. Any diagonal floor stabilizes that preconditioner alone, never the covariance model or output variances.

All variants use residual-corrected moments BqᵀZq + Zqᵀ(Bq−AZq), and the corresponding bilinear correction for the mean. Float32 inner actions are checked with fresh float64 residual actions. A hierarchy residual is still a residual of the represented hierarchy; independent full-kernel output gates control its approximation error. A locality residual likewise does not remove truncation error. Keep all failures, numerical tuning and the512-query audit visible.

Apply the coupled formulation to locality too, including a cached mixed-precision graph where it fits; do not give H² exclusive use of the improved conditioning strategy. Original contact-Schur paths remain available. This prototype stores the full scalar/vector cross block in O(NC) memory and retains a dense C-by-C scalar prior factor. It is a step toward issue128, not a solution of its C proportional to N scalability requirement. No full augmented dense covariance is stored or factored.

The independent augmented-Gaussian tests check nonzero scalar targets, covariance actions, positivity and marginal means/variances. They cover the scalar-noise contract and preconditioner variants. Physical parameters, observed-fidelity requirements and all numerical output gates remain unchanged.

The final comparison also gives the coupled formulation to dense-action CG. Its cached float32 normal matrix fits only when the cross block keeps a single float32 copy and queries are processed in blocks of32; the float64 cross residual is then regenerated in bounded tiles. The initially unblocked capacity failure remains in the record, and the fitting rerun supplies the competitive timing. Locality also receives a float32 AFN preconditioner and keeps an independent float64 sparse residual action. These controls prevent either the coupled formulation or mixed precision from being exclusive to H².
